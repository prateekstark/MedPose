Which model?

HMR2 estimates a body surface from a picture, or independently for each video frame. WHAM uses a sequence of at least 30 frames; this local adapter assumes a stationary camera. Video results follow the video player.

Neither model is established as “best” for your research population. They do not produce calibrated clinical joint angles, forces, contact events or diagnoses. A child's proportions, clothing and occlusion can affect a generic SMPL estimate. Missing or ambiguous person detections are not filled with invented poses.

No cloud service is configured. Licensed SMPL assets stay on this computer. Exported results contain estimated geometry; they do not include the source picture.
