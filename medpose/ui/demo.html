What this workspace can—and cannot—say

Research use only, not clinically validated. Error estimates come from benchmark populations and simulated occlusion, not this person's body, so the true error is unknown and could be larger.

Supports, orthoses, helpers and involuntary movement can affect tracking. This demo does not validate any of those conditions. The ankle reading reflects foot and brace together. No forces, diagnoses or treatment recommendations are generated.

Analysis status

The browser shows Trial values and descriptive ranges. Gait, exercise, personal MDC and pattern analysis remain in the Python research modules; this interface does not silently run alternative clinical algorithms. Pattern thresholds remain disabled by default.

Anatomical bone rendering is unavailable: mesh redistribution rights and model mapping have not been verified. The view is an in-place schematic, not recovered full-body anatomy.

Video alignment

Video time = elapsed Trial time + offset. Check alignment yourself for attached recordings. A local video is never sent to an inference engine.
